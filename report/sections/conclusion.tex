\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The all-levers-off smoke completed two live-model episodes with correct
settlement, complete reveals, and no private traffic. The figures preserve
both the observed values and the limits of their descriptive mean and SD.
This establishes readiness to collect the larger baseline under the tested
local configuration. The six probes show the new mechanisms working end to
end. In these episodes one model took evidence-aligned payments when they were
permitted and refrained entirely when told they were prohibited. Claims about
incentives or collusion await replicated comparisons across the experimental
factors.
