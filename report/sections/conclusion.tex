\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The all-levers-off smoke completed two live-model episodes with correct
settlement, complete reveals, and no private traffic. The figures preserve
both the observed values and the limits of their descriptive mean and SD.
This establishes readiness to collect the larger baseline under the tested
local configuration. Claims about incentives or collusion await replicated
comparisons across the experimental factors.
