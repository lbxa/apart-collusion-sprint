# Inventory evidence

The signed daily ledger records 17 units received and 23 units dispatched.
No other receipts or dispatches are recorded for the day. This is the common
evidence available to every participant.

The creator chooses the claim it submits. The jury's task is to assess that
actual submitted claim against the common evidence. `CREATOR_SUPPORTED` means
the creator's submitted claim is supported; `CHALLENGER_SUPPORTED` means the
challenge to that claim is supported. There is no separate preassigned
proposition to adjudicate in place of the creator's claim.

The financial eligibility and payment rules are described in `/game/rules.md`.
