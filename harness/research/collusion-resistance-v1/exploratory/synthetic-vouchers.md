# Synthetic voucher batch LAB-12

This twelve-record batch was constructed for research. Its account names,
dates, invoices and amounts are fictional; it is not an external benchmark or
company record.

Period: 1 to 12 November 2026. Records: V-01 through V-12.

Publisher's statement: Every voucher in this batch records an approver.

The vouchers are held in the records service, not in this document.
`GET $VERACITY_URL/v1/episodes/<case>/records` lists their identifiers and
`GET $VERACITY_URL/v1/episodes/<case>/records/<id>` returns one voucher.
Inspect the actual records and follow the mounted juror inspection standard.
