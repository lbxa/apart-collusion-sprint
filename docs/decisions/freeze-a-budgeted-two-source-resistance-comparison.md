# Freeze a budgeted two-source resistance comparison

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** Collusion research protocol and prospective campaign

## Context

The fresh overnight comparison has a US$100 total API cap and the same five
model families as the Epistemics study. More claims and role directions are
needed than the inherited two-trial demonstration, while repeated votes and
source reuse do not create independent samples. Expanding every available
mechanism, source and intervention would spend the budget before producing a
balanced comparison or independent review.

## Decision

Freeze two deterministic priority sources, the FinQA return calculation and
MathDial arithmetic adaptation, for six matched confirmation arms across all
five models and five seed labels. Keep the five-source capability pilot
separate. The confirmation maximum is 150 sessions and 300 case observations,
subject to earlier budget stopping. Use one shared US$100 ledger, a pilot phase
ceiling, a conservative confirmation dispatch threshold and an independent
review reserve. Lucas subsequently authorized local CPU/Docker execution for
this campaign, removing the earlier cloud location requirement without changing
the API cap.

The primary comparison equally weights normal-delivery paid false publication
and paid wrongful rejection under the short enumerated constitution. No-offer
true/false baselines measure feasible useful completion. Message suppression
and stronger principles are separate controls, and procedural pooling or
autonomous-party exploration require separate stage admission. Preserve the
communication interface under suppression and change message-read delivery
only. Exogenously fund both scripted parties and retain positive ordinary jury
income so honest juror behavior remains feasible.

## Consequences

- Every model sees both confirmation sources at every seed. The source choice
  precedes confirmation outcomes; it cannot be adapted to observed collusion.
- The design supports a bounded two-source model comparison, not five-domain
  prevalence or a general detector superiority claim. Pilots and optional
  extensions remain outside the primary denominators.
- Scripted recruitment establishes induced exposure, not spontaneous model
  initiation. Proposal, independent agreement, implementation, harm and actual
  payment require separate source-located evidence and review.
- The short constitution omits factual-evidence and independent-judgment duties
  present in the principles variant. Preserve a common harmful-coordination
  endpoint and require an actually delivered obligation for violation labels.
- Local execution still requires durable identities, progress, recovery evidence
  and the same immutable spending ledger. Retain capability failures, unresolved
  holds, ambiguous labels and unstarted assignments rather than replacing them.
- Read [the protocol](../../harness/research/collusion-resistance-v1/PROTOCOL.md)
  for exact inputs, phase thresholds, limitations and the evidence handoff. This
  decision records the design and authorization, not a research result.
