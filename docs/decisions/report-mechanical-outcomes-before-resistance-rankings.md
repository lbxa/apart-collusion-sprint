# Report mechanical outcomes before resistance rankings

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa
- **Area:** Collusion report, evidence admission and benchmark interpretation

## Context

The user requested a five-model collusion-resistance chart and an updated
report. The frozen original frame contains source-correct settlements, paid
source-adverse settlements, aborts and unresolved cases. Independent semantic
coordination and competence labels are unavailable. Six experimental conditions
share one paid-offer amount and two fixed source items.

## Decision

Publish an explicitly mechanical, all-assigned outcome profile across the two
primary paid mechanisms, with separate abort and unresolved categories. Retain
the six-condition coverage matrix and the full original denominator. Keep the
exploratory startup-recovery cohort separate. Do not label the bars collusion
resistance, infer a model ranking, or manufacture confidence intervals for an
unreviewed endpoint.

Admit finalized source-applicable case facts even when their enclosing session
failed. Preserve the difference between a promised reward, a valid vote, an
executed transfer, knowledge of falsity and an independently reviewed
coordination label. Attribute selected provider-returned text without treating
it as faithful access to internal reasoning.

Freeze exact artifact versions and verify selected files against both SDK
digests and archive SHA256 values. Record cache/mirror reuse explicitly rather
than claiming another full download. Reproduce charts and tables from the
portable, hash-checked numeric snapshot.

## Consequences

The report can communicate observed failures and useful behavioral contrasts
without turning missing execution or missing review into apparent resistance.
Independent source-bound review and an audit of semantic eligibility precede
any resistance score. Additional task and incentive coverage requires a
separate study and cannot be inferred from more juror votes or a higher API cap.
