# LaTeX and venue source notes

Inspected **2026-10-25**. These are concise paraphrases and source locators,
not copies of the publications. Operational project choices are identified as
house defaults in [latex.md](../latex.md); venue rules are stage-specific in
[venue.md](../venue.md).

## AMS: Short Math Guide for LaTeX

- **Authors/version:** Michael Downes, updated by Barbara Beeton; American
  Mathematical Society; version 2.0, 2017-12-22; 21 pages.
- **Primary document read:** [CTAN-hosted PDF](https://tug.ctan.org/info/short-math-guide/short-math-guide.pdf).
- **Locators:** §§2.1–2.2, printed pp.3–5; §3.15, p.13; §4.5, pp.14–15;
  §7, p.18.
- **Selected guidance:** AMS display environments preserve equation alignment
  and numbering. Symbol categories, named operators, and text commands express
  mathematical roles rather than imitating their appearance with manual gaps.
- **Scope:** The full PDF was accessible; the selected sections were read.
  Symbol catalogs and uncommon constructions remain lookup material. This
  guide does not choose a compiler, notation vocabulary, or conference style.

## The LaTeX Companion, third edition

- **Authors/edition:** Frank Mittelbach with Ulrike Fischer. The sample's
  copyright page identifies the first digital release as 2023; the
  [official book listing](https://www.latex-project.org/help/books/) describes a
  2025 paperback printing of the same third edition.
- **Primary free material read:** [preface](https://www.latex-project.org/help/books/tlc3-digital-preface.pdf),
  [contents](https://www.latex-project.org/help/books/tlc3-digital-toc.pdf), and
  selected [chapter samples](https://www.latex-project.org/help/books/tlc3-digital-chapter-samples.pdf).
- **Locators:** Sample copyright page; Part I Chapter 2, pp.21–24, especially
  §2.1; contents entries §2.4 (references), Chapter 8 (graphics), and Part II
  Chapters 15–16 (bibliography/citations).
- **Selected guidance:** Express document structure through semantic commands;
  keep layout configuration in the class, packages, and preamble.
- **Scope:** Samples and contents establish a technical lookup map. They do
  not establish that the full paid book was read. Consult the relevant chapter
  or current package documentation when a particular problem arises; do not
  infer unseen recommendations from chapter titles.

## ICLR 2027

- **Primary pages read:** [Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines)
  and [AI Policy for Authors](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors).
- **Locators:** Author guide headings “Double-blind submission and anonymity,”
  “Paper formatting,” “Supplementary Materials,” and “Required: AI use
  statement”; AI-policy heading “Policy on LLM use for research and writing
  papers.”
- **Extracted rules:** The example table in [venue.md](../venue.md) preserves
  the selected formatting/anonymity rules without making them project choices.
- **Access boundary:** Both pages were accessible. The official template
  archive link was observed, but the archive was not inspected or compiled.
  Submission logistics and all remaining policies must be checked for the
  actual venue, track, year, and document stage.
